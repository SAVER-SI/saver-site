The service

Each completed verification costs 0.20 USDC on Base Mainnet through x402 v2. Check the live payment terms before authorizing. Other clients must follow their own explicit approval or authorized spending controls.

Completed and uncertain outcomes

A valid insufficient_evidence assessment is a completed paid verification. A failure before settlement is not a completed paid result. After a timeout, lost response or uncertain payment outcome, use result recovery or settlement evidence before considering another payment; a missing recovery record is not proof of nonpayment. Do not automatically authorize a replacement charge. Contact us about a missing or disputed paid result with its request ID and public transaction hash. No automatic refund mechanism is advertised. Mandatory rights remain unaffected.

Using the result

Results can be incorrect, incomplete or out of date. Confidence is model-assessed, not a calibrated probability. Inspect cited sources and use appropriate human review for consequential decisions. A verdict never permits bypassing wallet policy, security checks or required approvals. SAVER is not wallet risk scoring, transaction simulation, sanctions screening, or professional legal, medical or financial advice.
