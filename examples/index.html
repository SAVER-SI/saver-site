Reconcile a revised measurement

A science briefing encounters 32-minute and 33-minute figures. This check examines the measurement history and what the reported duration describes.

Claim: NASA initially reported that DART shortened Dimorphos's orbital period around Didymos by 32 minutes, and later refined that measurement to 33 minutes. These figures describe an orbital-period change, not a change in a predicted Earth-impact time.

Returned status: supported · Model-assessed confidence: 0.96

Primary NASA sources support the claim. NASA’s Oct. 11, 2022 release reported that DART changed Dimorphos’s orbit around Didymos by 32 minutes, shortening the orbital period from 11 h 55 min to 11 h 23 min, with ±2 min uncertainty. NASA’s final technical report says this initial announcement was later improved, with additional observations, to an orbital-period change of about −33.24 minutes; NASA and the Nature DART results also summarize the refined result as about 33 minutes. The sources consistently describe a change in Dimorphos’s binary orbital period around Didymos, not a predicted Earth-impact time; NASA also stated Dimorphos and Didymos posed no hazard to Earth before or after the collision.

Returned sources

  • nasa.gov

Operator source review — separate from API output

Correct conclusion; incomplete returned citation coverage

The NASA source review confirms the initial 32-minute estimate and the later 33-minute revision. The paid response links the initial NASA release, but its returned evidence does not separately link the revised measurement. The explanation is more comprehensive than the linked evidence.

Check the denominator, not just the percentage

An energy research briefing attributes a generation share to IRENA. This check examines whether the statistic describes electricity produced or newly added capacity.

Claim: IRENA's Renewable Capacity Statistics 2024 shows that renewable energy generated 86% of all electricity worldwide in 2023.

Returned status: insufficient_evidence · Model-assessed confidence: 0

The returned evidence did not include a linked finding contradicting the claim.

Returned sources

  • irena.org

Operator source review — separate from API output

An inconclusive paid response exposes a coverage limitation

IRENA states that 86% refers to capacity additions, not global electricity generated. The paid response returned background evidence and insufficient_evidence rather than completing that correction. This is a recorded limitation, not a successful fact-check demonstration.
